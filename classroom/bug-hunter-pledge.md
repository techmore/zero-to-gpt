# The Bug Hunter Pledge 🐞🔍

*Read this together before you meet Buddy. Then everyone signs it.*

## What you should know first

- **Buddy is a computer program**, not a person. It doesn't think or feel, and it
  doesn't "know" things the way you do. It guesses words, one after another.
- **Buddy will sometimes be wrong ON PURPOSE.** Your teacher set it up that way so you
  can practice catching mistakes. That isn't a trick on you. It's the game, and now you know the rules.
- **Sometimes Buddy will be right.** You won't always know which is which. That's the point.
- **Real AI tools make mistakes by accident**, and they often sound very sure when
  they do. The skills you practice here work on every AI you'll ever use.
- **Buddy runs on a computer here at school.** What you type stays on that computer.
  It doesn't go out to the internet.

## My pledge

1. **I will check, not just trust.** When Buddy tells me something, I'll look for evidence.
2. **I will check, not just distrust.** "Buddy's always wrong" is lazy too. I'll prove it either way.
3. **I will write my expectation down first.** Then I'll compare it to what really happens.
4. **I will be kind to other hunters.** If my partner's test fails, we figure it out together.
   Everyone gets things wrong. That's how testing works.
5. **I will tell the truth about my results**, even when my test didn't catch anything.
6. **I won't type private stuff into Buddy**: no passwords, addresses, or secrets about
   me or anyone else.
7. **If something Buddy says feels wrong, mean, or confusing, I'll tell a teacher.**
